## What it does

**Corpse creation:** where a death is presented (the victim still spawned), a corpse captures:
- the pawn's displayed model and mesh matrix;
- its last local pose (`Character::displayPose`, read-only);
- velocity, chassis and damage type;
- headshot and the killer's position.

It is created on the main thread (`presentHook`), ticked per step in `joinStep`, and drawn with the pawn's draw owner and colours.

**Pick:** TnDeathFactory / TnDeathPicker over the authored `TR_Deaths_p` blueprints. The first entry in authored order whose Types are all active wins; none gives the default death (no animation, 1.0 s).
- **Robots:** HeadShot, Hovering, Airborne, and the momentum type. Momentum is the push away from the killer in the pawn's frame (PROV until the hit momentum is kept). Then the damage type's DamageDeathType (87 damage types resolved through the class hierarchy).
- **Vehicles:** Car / Tank + Moving (over 0.5 m/s) or Stationary; Plane.
- **TotalTime:** a random value in [Min, Max], or TotalTimeForAI when authored.

**Animation:**
- The entry's AnimationName is an **AnimSet chooser group** (e.g. ProjectileSingle → `Death_ProjectileSing_*`, weighted). The pick keeps only the clips the chassis' model has.
- Else it is a clip of that name.
- Else none: the pose holds, as the original's failed lookup would.
- It plays from the last pose over BlendInTime.

**Modifiers:**
- HeadShot: the head bone scales to 0.1.
- Melee: the Defrag energy-death sweep over 3 s, through the renderer's `setDrawEnergyDeath`.
- PlayEffect: Exp_Death_Sm_FX at the head and the body (robots), TF_Death_Buildup / MED_FX (vehicles).
- The **death light now comes from the same pick**, so body and light agree.

**Gib at TotalTime:**
- the body hides;
- Exp_Death_FX (robot), TF_Death_MED_FX (car / tank) or Exp_Death_Lrg_FX (jet);
- 4 RB_Gib chunks: ballistic and spinning, ground-clamped, 5 s, TF_Death_GibsTrail_FX.
- **Vehicles also leave the hull wreck:** the form's DefaultSkeletalGibTemplate, 34-chassis table. It takes TransferVelocityMultiplier × the velocity plus an upward kick, falls and settles (rigid; PROV no per-bone physics), and the Defrag dissolve starts after U(0.5, 1) s over 4.5 s (jets 6 s); life U(3, 5) s (jets 7).

**Death camera:** unchanged. The local camera already orbits the dead pawn's position, which is where the corpse is.
