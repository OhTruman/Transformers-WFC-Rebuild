## What was broken in your playtest and what changed

Your 43 findings are in docs/handoff/USER_PLAYTEST_2026-10-23b.md. The menu-side status per item is in
docs/handoff/HANDOVER_MENUS_2026-10-24.md (fixed / base game as authored / open). The big ones:

- **Nobody could move (every class, every mode).** A match you hosted alone was launched as an ONLINE match, which
  made your own player a network seat with no driver: the camera turned, the body stayed. Fixed (f0d500cc): a solo
  host runs the local loop. Also the local pawn was being occlusion-culled, which was the Scout "flicker" (fixed).
- **Escalation opened the Team Deathmatch lobby.** A "host now" override replaced Host in every party lobby. Fixed;
  Escalation uses its own lobby, keeps your lobby pick into the match (no second character screen), and plays.
- **Campaign Crash Site: blank screen after picking Megatron.** The solo campaign lobby is invisible by design in the
  original; the original's lobby code starts the chapter the moment everyone has a character, ours never did, so you
  sat in an invisible lobby. Fixed: the chapter starts on the pick, the pick carries into the map, a loading bar shows
  progress. All ten chapters load and reach a checkpoint headless; A4 had a real start-of-chapter death from a
  hazard-volume test (fixed).
- **Menu traps / wrong character screens / Private Match without Game Settings.** Fixed; the pause menu, Change
  Character, Game Settings, Back and the countdown all route correctly (your flows below prove it).
- **Controllers in matches.** Buttons only reached the menus; now the authored Xbox layout drives the match (A jump,
  B grenade, X interact / reload, Y weapon, LS transform, RS melee, RT fire, LB ability, RB dash, D-pad left
  killstreak; the two original control-scheme options apply).
- **Settings.** Every graphics row was measured to apply what it says (docs/handoff/QC_RENDER_SETTINGS_AUDIT.md);
  settings survive a renderer recreate; Texture Quality is live; menus are held at 60 FPS; the brightness
  calibration runs at the first launch (once) and its logos respond to the slider.
- **Your new rows.** Scroll arrows on Graphics; "Apply the changed graphics settings?" confirm; a result popup
  (Restart Required / next map / Settings Applied); Private Match team row + the Back-cancels-countdown rule;
  voice-channel key (N / D-pad Right); Language (English / French); Split Screen Layout; Subtitles.
